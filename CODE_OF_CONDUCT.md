# Code of Conduct

For the remainder of this document, "I" will refer to "Typeir".

I will be terse. Don't be evil. Don't be a dick.

## My Pledge

To make cool software, not generic slop.

## My Standards

Any contribution has to meaningfully increase the quality of the target software, or it has to introduce a human-designed, creative angle to an already existing element.

All of the contributions have to follow the project standards, as defined in the architecture files, and every external contribution request has to hold an explanation of every change in the request itself. If any of the claims is not backed up by the code, the contribution will be rejected, and you will be penalized. Multiple of these will result in a permanent ban from any future contribution.

For external contributors, coverage must be 100%, and the following testing disciplines have to be covered, to the best of their ability and applicability:

- unit testing
- e2e testing
- integration testing
- mutation testing

Any project with a visual component, additionally, will need a pixel-diffed visual test for deterministic UI elements.
If your work takes a bounded, discrete range as input, then your test must cover its edges and average value.
In essence, do a _boundary value analysis_; otherwise it will be rejected no matter the coverage.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

You are responsible for enforcing the standards to the best of your ability. If you cannot enforce them, do not contribute.

## Scope

This is a personal project. The scope is whatever I decide when I wake up. Feel free to contribute or reach out though.

## Enforcement

I am the sole enforcer of the code of conduct, and reserve my rights to revoke it or act upon any arbitrary rule at any time.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Whichever I decide on that day.

### Correction

Just do what I ask you to do if I ever reject a contribution, and follow the standards again.

### Warning

Consider any rejection a warning. I may or may not supply information for it.

### Temporary Ban

There are no temporary bans.

### Permanent Ban

A twice-rejected contribution will result in a permanent ban. Two separate rejected contributions by the same author or coauthor will result in all the coauthors being banned too.

## Attribution

Always attribute work to its respective owners. You can attribute work to LLMs. If you use a popular, authored algorithm such as Bayer's dithering or Perlin's noise, attribute it to them.
